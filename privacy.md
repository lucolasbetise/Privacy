# Everything Fits – Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 27 November 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Everything Fits ("the App") is a mobile puzzle game developed and operated by Alphabetise (Luke) ("we", "us" or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and shared when you use the App.

Everything Fits is designed to operate primarily offline, without requiring users to create an account.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Locally Stored Information

The App stores certain information directly on your device to provide its functionality, including:

- Game progress and completed levels.
- Unlocked chapters and collected objects.
- Available hints and earned rewards.
- Daily rewards and login streaks.
- Audio, accessibility and gameplay preferences.
- Premium purchase status.
- Advertising preferences and frequency information.

This information is stored locally on your device and is not routinely transmitted to servers operated by the developer.

### 2.2 Advertising Information

Everything Fits uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may collect and process information including:

- Device identifiers.
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
- IP addresses.
- Device type and operating system information.
- Advertising impressions and interactions.
- Approximate location derived from network information.
- Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and maintain advertising services.

The developer does not directly receive or control all information collected by Google.

Further information about Google's data processing practices is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. Advertising

The free version of Everything Fits may display:

- Interstitial advertisements between eligible completed puzzles.
- Optional rewarded advertisements that provide in-game hints.

Advertisements are not required to complete the game's standard puzzles.

Users may purchase a permanent Premium upgrade that removes advertisements from the App, including optional rewarded advertisements.

Advertising services require an internet connection. Standard gameplay remains available offline.

## 4. Advertising Consent

Everything Fits uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices where applicable.

Users located in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, Switzerland or other applicable jurisdictions may be presented with a privacy consent message.

Depending on applicable legal requirements, users may be able to:

- Consent to personalised advertising.
- Decline consent.
- Manage advertising privacy preferences.
- Withdraw previously provided consent.

Where consent is required, advertising requests will be made in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

On Apple devices, permission to track users across other companies' apps and websites will be requested through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework where applicable.

Declining tracking permission does not prevent access to standard gameplay.

## 5. In-App Purchases

Everything Fits may offer optional in-app purchases, including:

- A permanent Premium upgrade that removes advertisements.
- Additional in-game hint packs.

Purchases made through the Apple App Store are processed by Apple.

We do not directly collect or store your credit card information, banking details or Apple Account password.

Purchase identifiers and transaction information may be processed to verify purchases, deliver purchased content and restore eligible purchases.

Apple's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## 6. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App and its integrated services may be used to:

- Provide and maintain gameplay functionality.
- Save game progress and preferences.
- Deliver advertisements where permitted.
- Provide earned and purchased rewards.
- Verify and restore eligible purchases.
- Prevent fraud and misuse.
- Diagnose technical issues.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not directly sell users' personal information.

Third-party advertising providers may process information for advertising purposes in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable consent requirements.

## 7. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar legislation applies, relevant information may be processed on the following legal bases:

- **Consent:** Where required for advertising, tracking or access to information stored on a device.
- **Contractual necessity:** To provide requested functionality and purchased content.
- **Legitimate interests:** Where appropriate, to maintain security, prevent fraud and support essential application functionality.
- **Legal obligations:** Where processing is required by applicable law.

Users may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information is retained on your device to maintain gameplay progress and preferences.

You may remove locally stored game information by deleting the App, subject to any device backups or operating system storage mechanisms.

Information processed by Google or Apple is retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

Users may contact us regarding personal information held directly by the developer.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Everything Fits is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Where applicable, advertising and data processing must comply with relevant children's privacy legislation and platform requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information has been collected from their child may contact us using the details provided below.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

- Request deletion of personal information.
